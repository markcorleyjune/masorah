›How do I navigate to a passage?
Click the passage picker (e.g. "Gen 1") in the reader toolbar. Select a book, then a chapter, then optionally type a verse number.
›How do I select texts?
Click "ESV · THOT" in the toolbar to open the full text selector. Choose manuscripts, translations, and witnesses from all T01–T05 tiers.
›How does the word hover work?
Click any Hebrew or English word. A popup shows: pronunciation, Strong's number, Webster 1828 definition, occurrence count, and full morphology.
›What is THOT?
THOT = Translators Hebrew Old Testament. The primary manuscript witness in this corpus, with full Masoretic pointing (vowels + cantillation marks).
›How do I search?
Click ⌛ to open the floating search bar. Search Hebrew, transliteration, or English. Use quotes for exact phrases.
›How do I add more panes?
Click + in the toolbar to add a new reading pane. Each pane can display a different text or version.
›Where do exports go?
Click the folder icon once to choose a real destination folder (File System Access API) — the choice is remembered, and Export then writes the current passage (Hebrew + WEB + lexicon) as JSON straight there. Without a chosen folder it tries the local engine, then falls back to a browser download.
