⚖ Coherence Logic — Masoretic Code

Priority 1 — Highest

Baseline Convergence & Transmissional Coherence

Readings with greatest stable agreement across traceable transmission streams represent the most probable ancestral state. The 05c baseline (Ben Ḥayyim 1525) is the primary coherence anchor. Higher coherence = more likely authentic continuity. Stable, traceable manuscript agreement takes priority over earlier or reconstructed witnesses.

Priority 2 — Tie-Break

Scribal Probability

Prefer readings that best explain the emergence of competing variants through known scribal processes: harmonization, assimilation, omission, smoothing, expansion. The reading that most economically explains its rivals is preferred.

Priority 3 — Final Tie-Break

Lectio Difficilior

The more difficult reading is preferred only when coherence and transcriptional probability remain functionally equal. Applied as final arbiter only. Scribes simplify; they rarely complicate.
